Frequently Asked Questions

✧
What types of spaces can you design?
You can enquire about kitchens, living rooms, dining areas, bedrooms, bathrooms, workspaces and complete home interiors.
Can I discuss my budget before starting?
Yes. Share your estimated budget and requirements so the project scope and possible design options can be discussed.
Can I request a customised design?
Yes. Use the enquiry form to describe your preferred style, room dimensions if available, and any design references you like.
How do I arrange a consultation?
Call 8891155322 or use the WhatsApp enquiry button to start a conversation about your project.
Can I see actual completed projects?
Ask Design Castle for photographs of completed projects, relevant project details and customer references before making a decision.
